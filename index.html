01 About

Research at the intersection of vision and language.

I am a Research Scientist at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), where I work on visual perception and multimodal intelligence.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UT Austin, advised by Prof. Philipp Krähenbühl. Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Cornell University, where I worked with Prof. Bharath Hariharan and Prof. Kavita Bala. I have also worked at NVIDIA and Meta FAIR.
