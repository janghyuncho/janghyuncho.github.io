Jang Hyun (Vincent) Cho

Member of Technical Staff · Thinking Machines Lab

I work on multimodal research at Thinking Machines Lab. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Meta Superintelligence Labs.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UT Austin, advised by Prof. Philipp Krähenbühl. Before that, I received my B.S. from Cornell University, where I worked with Prof. Bharath Hariharan and Prof. Kavita Bala. Earlier, I also worked at Meta FAIR and NVIDIA.
