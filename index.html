Jang Hyun (Vincent) Cho

Research Scientist · Meta Superintelligence Labs

I am on the multimodal pretraining team, building the next generation of Muse Spark.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UT Austin, advised by Prof. Philipp Krähenbühl. Before that, I received my B.S. from Cornell University, where I worked with Prof. Bharath Hariharan and Prof. Kavita Bala. I have previously worked at Meta FAIR and NVIDIA.
